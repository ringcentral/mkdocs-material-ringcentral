## The role of content in design

UI copy isn't a handoff deliverable — it's a design decision made alongside visual and interaction choices. Copy shapes how users understand the product, how they feel about it, and whether they trust it.

**Start with the user's goal.** Every label, heading, and message should serve the user's task, not explain the system to them.

**Be specific.** Vague copy creates anxiety. "An error occurred" is worse than "We couldn't save your changes — check your network connection and try again."

**Earn every word.** If removing a word doesn't change the meaning, remove it.
